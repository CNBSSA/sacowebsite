About Us

Books Xperts (Pty) Ltd

Books Xperts (Pty) Ltd is a South African software company building practical business tools for local entrepreneurs and SMEs. We are based in South Africa and all four of our products are designed specifically for the South African regulatory environment.

Our belief is simple: South African businesses deserve software that works the way South Africa works — with SARS-compliant calculations, CIPC-ready workflows, and ZAR pricing that reflects local economic reality. We do not adapt international products and hope the edge cases do not matter. We build for SA from the start.

Whether you are a sole proprietor managing your own books, an HR manager running payroll for 200 staff, an attorney administering family trusts, or an entrepreneur registering your first company — we have built something for you.
